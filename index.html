Import into your recipe app

Copy a recipe's link and paste it into the import field of Instant Connect / Fresco, Paprika, Mela, Crouton or AnyList. The ingredients, timings and every step arrive already filled in — no retyping, and nothing to copy by hand.

This works because each page carries its recipe a second time in schema.org/Recipe form, as machine-readable data embedded in the page itself. That is the same format Google reads to show a recipe card in search results, so most apps understand it without a plugin.

On a Pro Max you can send an imported recipe to the cooker from Instant Connect and let it drive the programme, rather than setting each stage by hand.

How these recipes are written

Calibrated for one cooker
Timings assume a 6 qt Instant Pot Pro Max (1200 W), which preheats faster and runs hotter on Sauté than a Duo. Where that matters, the recipe says so and gives you the adjustment.
Every step names its setting
Each instruction is tagged with the function, level and duration — Sauté · Medium · 8 min — so you are never guessing which button to press or how long to hold it there.
Steps that stand alone
A step continuing an existing Sauté session repeats that context instead of assuming you remember it. Recipe apps read each step in isolation, so this is what keeps the appliance settings intact after an import.
Weights, not cups
Ingredients are given in grams and millilitres. In a sealed pot the ratio of rice to water decides the result, and a cup of rice is not a reliable amount.
The failure modes, up front
Where a step commonly goes wrong — a Burn warning from an unscraped base, spices catching on High — the recipe says what happens and why, at the point where it matters.
