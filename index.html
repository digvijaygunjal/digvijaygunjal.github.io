Instant Pot · One pot · Vegetarian

Masala Rice

Whole spices bloomed in oil, potatoes, cauliflower, peanuts and peas, all pressure cooked with basmati rice in five minutes.

Prep15 min Cook25 min Total40 min Serves4 CuisineIndian

Ingredients

Whole spices & fat

  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 tsp mustard seeds
  • 2 pinches asafoetida
  • 4 whole cloves
  • 2 star anise
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 4 whole black peppercorns

Aromatics

  • 15 g green chillies, finely chopped
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 onions, finely chopped

Ground spices

  • 1 tbsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tbsp ground coriander
  • 1 tsp red chilli powder, or to taste
  • 1 tbsp garam masala
  • 1.5 tsp salt

Vegetables & rice

  • 2 small potatoes, thinly diced
  • 80 g cauliflower florets, chopped
  • 50 g raw peanuts
  • 80 g green peas
  • 300 g basmati rice, washed and drained
  • 400 ml water

To finish

  • 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped

Instructions

  1. Preheat on SautéSet the Instant Pot to Sauté on High for 12 minutes. Wait until the display reads Hot, then add 2 tbsp oil to the inner pot.Sauté · High · 12 min
  2. Bloom the whole spicesAdd the cumin seeds, mustard seeds, asafoetida, cloves, star anise, bay leaf and black peppercorns to the hot oil. Stir for 30 seconds, until the mustard seeds pop and the spices smell fragrant.
  3. Cook the aromatics and onionsAdd the green chillies and ginger-garlic paste and stir for 45 seconds, keeping it moving so it does not stick. Add the onions and cook for 5 to 6 minutes, stirring regularly, until translucent and soft.
  4. Roast the ground spicesAdd the turmeric, ground coriander, red chilli powder, garam masala and salt. Stir constantly for 30 seconds to roast the spices without letting them catch.
  5. Add the vegetables and peanutsAdd the diced potatoes, cauliflower florets, peanuts and green peas. Sauté for 2 minutes, stirring, until everything is coated in the spice mixture.
  6. Toast the riceAdd the washed and thoroughly drained basmati rice. Stir gently for 1 minute to coat and lightly toast the grains, taking care not to break them.
  7. Deglaze and cancel SautéPour in 400 ml water. Scrape the bottom of the pot thoroughly with a wooden spoon to lift every browned spice bit, or the cooker will show a Burn warning. Level the rice so it sits under the liquid, then press Cancel to turn off Sauté.Cancel
  8. Pressure cookLock on the lid and turn the valve to Sealing. Pressure cook on High for 5 minutes.Pressure Cook · High · 5 min
  9. Natural release, then ventWhen cooking finishes, leave the pot undisturbed for a 3 minute natural pressure release. Then turn the valve to Venting to release the remaining steam, and wait for the float valve to drop before opening.Natural release · 3 min, then quick release
  10. Fluff, garnish and serveOpen the lid away from you. Remove the bay leaf and star anise. Fluff the rice gently with a fork, fold through the chopped fresh coriander and serve hot.

Salt is set at 1.5 tsp. The original draft called for 1.5 tbsp, which is roughly 27 g for 300 g of rice and would be inedibly salty — almost certainly a tsp/tbsp slip. Taste after fluffing and add more if needed.
